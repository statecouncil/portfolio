Hi, I'm Paul.

I'm a level designer and student of Game Design and Development at Macromedia University of Applied Sciences in Cologne, Germany. My focus is on level design and environmental storytelling, with additional work in tabletop RPG design, game writing, and scripting.

My shipped work includes officially licensed Team Fortress 2 maps. This portfolio presents selected projects and retrospectives from before, during, and after my Game Design studies.
